I would also freeze the terminology

From this point onwards, I'd consistently refer to the platform areas as:

Area	Meaning
Registry	The permanent record and provenance infrastructure
Studio	The authenticated workspace where participants manage their records
Field	The public discovery, verification, and opportunity layer
Archive	Historical records and documentation within a participant's Studio
Stewardship	The ongoing lifecycle of records after their creation

Using those terms consistently—in documentation, code comments, product copy, and conversations—will reinforce the mental model you've been building.
